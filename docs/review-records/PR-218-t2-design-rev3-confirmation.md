  * **Medium P1 (plans with lines are refused only in application code).** Sections 4.7 and 13 make CI's journal check an activation condition "for plans with lines", and say that until then E3 refuses such plans. But the refusal lives in E3's `ICompensationJournalWriter`, and activation is a single runtime INSERT grant on the execution tables, not a per-plan-type switch. With the runtime credentials plus an intercepted grant for a plan with lines (the exact threat row of table 7.1), an attacker executes directly in SQL: the epoch is bound and verifies, but the compensation journals are not bound at all (none, or any amounts), so the general-ledger side of the replay is unauthorised. The read side (section 9) compares the epoch text, not the journals.
    * Resolution: move the journal check into slice A as a deferred database check from day one: for a plan with lines, the multiset of (entry date, period, branch, account, debit, credit) of the GL journal lines linked to this execution through 0036's compensation-journal table equals the signed plan lines; a plan with no lines must have no compensation journals. Until CI writes journals, every plan with lines then fails this check in the database, which is the intended refusal, and CI needs no later change to the trigger (the guard forbids `CREATE OR REPLACE`). Also: the offline tool and the (ii) signer refuse to sign a plan with lines while CI is not built, and the read side compares journals with the signed lines. Add tests: an execution of a plan with lines with missing, extra or altered journals is refused at commit; a plan without lines with any compensation journal is refused.
  * **Low P2 (layer remaining figures and operation metadata are not in the epoch text).** Section 4.4 says remaining layer quantities "are not listed separately: 0014 derives them from the layers and consumption rows". 0014 does not derive them; it only bounds them to `[0, original]`. After the epoch, the remaining figures of the layers it created, consumed or restored drive future FIFO cost, and they are not bound by the grant. The operation rows of the epoch members (source, kind, date, document references) are bound only to the extent 0036's member roles, lineage and X7 constrain them; the text does not say so. The runtime role can already write layer remaining figures outside T2, so this is not a T2 escalation, but the claim must be true.
    * Resolution: add one `R|<item>|<warehouse>|<layer seq>|<remaining quantity>|<remaining amount>` line per layer the epoch creates, consumes or restores, after the epoch (ordered as `L`), with a test; or correct the sentence and state that the remaining figures and the operation metadata are bounded by 0014 and 0036 but not signed. Add a schema-pinning test so a new column on the 0014 entry, layer or consumption tables fails until it is classified as signed or excluded.
