# HASM — Phase 3.7C: Reversal, derived accounting states and the inventory valuation close probe (as built)

Third slice of Phase 3.7 (after 3.7A configuration and 3.7B posting). Sources: SRS 5.5, 9.5, 9.6, 19.2, 19.3, 19.5, scenario 21.4; ARCHITECTURE 5, 7, 8.

* **Company migration 0017** (`CompanySchema.StockReversal.cs`): `StockPostingReversal` (one row per reversed posting: compensating costing operation, JournalAction 0 = no journal, 1 = nothing posted left to undo (the unposted entry was cancelled, or it was already cancelled or reversed outside), 2 = a draft reversal entry was created for a posted entry; effective date, who, when, reason). Append-only; runtime role SELECT and INSERT only. **System migration 0019:** permission `company.stockdocs.reverse` (warehouse-scoped, Company Administrator preset).
* **Reversal** (`POST /api/v1/company/stock-documents/{id}/reversal`, body effectiveDate and reason): one company transaction takes the stock effect back through the costing engine (compensating entries: an outflow gets back exactly what it consumed; an inflow is taken out again only while that stock is untouched, otherwise `reversal_not_possible_*` and nothing changes), then handles the accounting side by its actual state: an entry that is Draft, Pending or Approved is cancelled; a Posted entry gets a **draft reversal entry** that follows the ordinary approval and posting workflow (the ledger is never edited); a Cancelled or Reversed entry (changed outside the stock reversal) needs nothing more, which also lets a posting whose accounting failed be repaired. The effective date must lie in an open fiscal period and not before the posting date; **while the entry is not posted the reversal must stay in the posting's fiscal period** (otherwise that period could never agree with the ledger). A posting is reversed once; the identical retry returns the original; simultaneous requests reverse exactly once; the document is never edited or cancelled.
* **Accounting state, derived from facts** (never a stored flag that can drift): 1 pending (a draft journal entry awaits approval and posting), 2 no journal required, 3 posted, 4 **failed** (the journal entry was cancelled or reversed outside the stock reversal; blocks the close until the stock is reversed), 5 reversed. The Posting tab of the stock document shows it (Phase 3.7D).
* **Real `inventory_valuation_differences` close probe** (replaces the "module absent" placeholder): a period cannot be closed while a posting that is not reversed has a cancelled or reversed journal entry, or while the sum of the costing ledger up to the period end differs from the balance of the inventory accounts in the General Ledger (posted and reversed entries) up to that date. Draft entries are additionally caught by the existing pending-entries probe. **Known limitation (accepted, resolved by Phase 3.27 opening stock):** once a stock posting exists, a ledger balance on an inventory account that the costing ledger does not know about (an opening balance kept only in the ledger, a manual entry) counts as a difference.
* **Refactor without behaviour change:** `JournalService.CancelAsync/ReverseAsync` keep their validation, revision check and transaction; their bodies became the internal `CancelInWorkAsync/ReverseInWorkAsync` so the stock reversal can run them inside its own transaction.
