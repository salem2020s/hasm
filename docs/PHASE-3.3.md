`# Phase 3.3 — Financial reporting and Excel/PDF exports (as built)
`
`Approved by the owner (scope proposal `docs/PHASE-3.3-PROPOSAL.md`, requirements `docs/REPORTING-REQUIREMENTS.md`). One branch, `feature/phase-3-3-financial-reporting`, logical commits per slice, verified checkpoints pushed automatically (policy in `CLAUDE.md`). Slices are added to this file as they are built.
`
`## Owner decisions (2026-11-15)
`1. **Statement classification** is a configurable, company-level, versioned and audited mapping that is logically separate from the Chart of Accounts (S3). An unclassified account never disappears silently; statements with unresolved classification are marked incomplete; changing the mapping creates a new version and never silently reinterprets an earlier run.
`2. **Libraries:** an MIT-licensed OpenXML writer for XLSX (S4); the PDF engine is chosen by a proof of concept behind an interface, with the licensing assessment recorded (S5). QuestPDF's conditional license is not assumed to be acceptable.
`3. **Limits:** XLSX/CSV default maximum 500,000 rows (configurable, `Hasm:Reports:MaxExportRows`), PDF has its own limit (`MaxPdfRows`, default 50,000); an export above a limit is refused BEFORE a file is produced; there is no fixed five-year range limit (configurable sanity bound `MaxRangeYears`, default 100).
`4. **Comparatives:** previous fiscal period and previous fiscal year, actual values only, respecting the company's own fiscal calendar.
`5. **Permissions:** statements have their own permissions, distinct from the ledger-report ones (system migration 0014): `company.financialstatements.view|export` (branch- and cost-center-scoped) and `company.statementmapping.view|configure` (company-wide).
`6. The full visual Report Designer remains a mandatory future Release 1 deliverable; report definitions here only prepare for it.
`
`## S1 — Reporting foundation (built)
`* **Report model** (`Hasm.Infrastructure.Reporting`): a neutral `ReportDocument` (bilingual titles, header lines, typed columns, a STREAMED row sequence, row count known before rendering, notices) that every renderer consumes; `ReportDefinitions` registry with stable report ids, parameters, columns, permissions and formats (the future designer builds on it). Endpoints: `GET /api/v1/company/reports/definitions` (only reports the caller may view, with `canExport`) and `GET /api/v1/company/reports/period`.
`* **Shared export service** (`ReportExportService`): column selection (`columns=` keys), format registry (CSV now; XLSX and PDF plug in as renderers), explicit row limits, a short-lived temporary file deleted when the response ends (no partial download is ever sent), and an audit record for every outcome (`report.exported`, `export_refused`, `export_failed`; event types 1110-1112) carrying report id, format, row count, size, outcome and a compact non-sensitive filter summary, never the contents.
`* **Fix of the silent truncation:** the General Ledger CSV used to stop after 5,000 lines. Exports now stream the COMPLETE authorized result from one consistent snapshot (`LedgerReportService.OpenGeneralLedgerExportAsync`); the interactive page keeps its explicit 5,000-line page size; above the export limit the request is refused with `export_too_large`. Regression test with 5,600 lines.
`* **Fiscal period resolution** (`ReportPeriods`): fiscal year, fiscal period or custom range (exactly one), with precise comparison rules. Previous period: the period that ends the day before (it may be in the previous fiscal year); previous year: the year that ends the day before, the period with the same number (unavailable when that year is divided differently), or a custom range moved back exactly 12 months (month ends preserved, impossible days clamped). A missing counterpart is reported as unavailable with a reason; nothing is invented. Works for non-calendar fiscal years.
`* **Authorization:** export needs the separate export permission; branch and cost-center restrictions are applied inside the queries before the document exists (an export never contains more than the caller may see); the old fixed 5-year limit is replaced by the configurable sanity bound.
`* **Permissions and migration:** system migration `0014_financial_reporting_access` adds the four permission codes to the Company Administrator preset.
`
`## S2 — General Ledger and Trial Balance completion (built)
`* **Same engine:** every figure comes from `LedgerReportService` (one consistent snapshot, posted/reversed entries, authorization applied inside the query). No second ledger engine exists. The trial balance can now be requested for a fiscal year, a fiscal period or a date range (`fiscalYearId`, `fiscalPeriodId`, `from`/`to`), with `comparison=previous_period|previous_year`, \`maxLevel\` (depth) and a deterministic \`sort\` (code, name, closing balance; ties by code) among siblings.
`* **Comparatives** are computed by running the same engine over the comparative range with the SAME authorization scope (a caller never sees more in the comparison column than in the main one) and merged by account identity; a comparison without a counterpart is reported as unavailable with a reason (see S1 rules; quarters are never compared with months). Parents are never double counted (leaf figures roll up once).
`* **Reconciliation** (\`GET /reports/reconciliation\`): on ONE snapshot, the trial balance is checked against the general ledger's own line-by-line running balances for every postable account, group roll-ups equal the sum of their children, debits equal credits (not applicable to a branch/cost-center restricted view, which legitimately shows part of a document) and every opening balance equals the previous day's closing. A failing check lists the accounts and amounts; nothing is adjusted. The checks are a pure function with unit tests that prove each kind of inconsistency is detected.
`* **General ledger:** fiscal selectors; drill-down from a line to its journal entry (the entry screen opens the entry; the server still authorizes it); \`grouping=month\` adds monthly subtotal rows to exports (the row limit uses an upper bound so it is never under-counted).
`* **UI:** one shared screen (Web and Windows Desktop): period mode, comparison, depth, sort, visible-column choice (screen and export), exports in the formats the server offers, reconciliation panel.
`
`## S3 — Financial statements and classification (built)
`* **Classification design.** The Chart of Accounts stays the accounting structure. The presentation is a SEPARATE company-level mapping of accounts to statement sections (current/non-current assets and liabilities, capital, retained earnings, other equity, operating revenue, revenue reductions, cost of sales, direct service costs, operating expenses, other income, finance and other expenses). Accounts are referenced by identity (never by code, so re-coding or moving an account keeps its classification). A group's section applies to its whole subtree and a descendant's own entry wins, so nothing is counted twice. PostgreSQL enforces which sections fit an account type.
`* **Versioning and audit.** Company migration \`0011_company_statement_classification\`: \`StatementMappingVersion\` and \`StatementMappingEntry\`, immutable (update, delete and truncate are refused by triggers). Every save creates a new version holding the COMPLETE mapping (an unchanged mapping creates nothing), with optimistic concurrency by version number, an advisory lock and an audit event (type 1113). A statement can be requested with any earlier version (\`mappingVersion\`), so a later classification change never reinterprets an earlier run, and every statement and export states the version used.
`* **Defaults.** Derived from the starter templates (generic, service, retail, wholesale) for template accounts only; a user-created account is never assumed classified. A suggestion is loaded into the form and saved only by an explicit action. A contra revenue account (a revenue account with a debit nature) defaults to revenue reductions.
`* **Unclassified never disappears.** An account with a balance and no section appears in its own "unclassified" block (counted in the totals), the statement is marked INCOMPLETE (banner, issue, failed check, notice in exports); without any mapping every account is unclassified and the statement says so.
`* **Statements.** Profit and Loss (operating revenue, revenue reductions, net revenue, cost of sales, direct service costs, gross profit only when those are classified, operating expenses, operating profit, other income, finance and other expenses, profit or loss), Balance Sheet (current/non-current assets and liabilities, equity, retained earnings, and the **derived** result of the revenue and expense accounts not yet closed to equity: zero after the year-end closing entries, equal to the open years' result before them, never a posted entry), and Trading Account / Cost of Sales (applies only when accounts are classified as cost of sales; direct service costs never make it applicable; nothing is inferred from inventory).
`* **Historical correctness.** The Profit and Loss and the Trading Account use period activity with the year-end closing entries excluded (they only move the result to equity); the Balance Sheet uses cumulative balances at the date including closing entries and carried-forward history. Tested before and after closing, across consecutive years, after reversing a closing and after re-closing. Comparatives use the same rules over the comparative range.
`* **Checks shown with every statement:** classification complete, result/net sales/balance-sheet totals agree with the trial balance figures, and the accounting equation (not applicable to a restricted view).
`* **Authorization.** \`company.financialstatements.view|export\` (branch and cost-center scoped like the ledger reports, applied inside the queries before anything is built; separate from the ledger-report permissions) and \`company.statementmapping.view|configure\` (company-wide). Exports need the export permission, contain only what the caller may see and are audited.
`* **UI.** Shared screens for the statements and for the classification (tree of accounts, allowed sections only, template suggestion, versions, concurrency message), Arabic and English.
