## 1. Sources and scope

* **Approved scope (ROADMAP 3.13):** SRS 4.3, 4.4, 7.2, 7.3, 7.6, 19.2, 19.3, 19.7, 21.1. Prerequisites 3.7C, 3.9B, 3.10, 3.11 are merged.
* **Deliverables (ROADMAP):** shared life cycle with three independent tracks (commercial status, physical delivery/receipt, accounting posting); links between documents; partial fulfilment; numbering per type/branch/year; approval and re-approval; snapshot storage; idempotency keys; reuse of the 3.7 accounting-pending mechanism; a shared posted-business-transaction counting hook (no-op until Phase 4.2 gives it the Trial definition of OD-12); document-level foreign-currency rule (documents may be created and posted at their dated document rate; settling or allocating across currencies or rates is refused, fail closed, until 3.18).
* **Acceptance (ROADMAP):** a business event creates **exactly one stock and one ledger effect**; separate and direct modes both supported. **Mandatory tests:** PostgreSQL: duplicate prevention under concurrency, state machine, numbering, approval. Also Release 1 acceptance B.1 (scenario 21.1) and C (invariants SRS 19.2, 19.3, 19.7, 19.8 under concurrency).
* **What the SRS says (quoted in substance):**
  * 7.2: two modes per operation: a **direct invoice** creates the required stock effect, or a **separate delivery/receipt** with partial shipments and links between documents; stock movement and ledger effect are never duplicated between the invoice and the delivery/receipt document.
  * 7.3: in direct mode the physical event is recorded when the invoice that contains it is approved and the stock moves once; in separate mode the stock moves when the delivery/receipt document is approved, not when the invoice is approved. In both modes the accounting effect is posted automatically or stays in an **explicit accounting-pending state** with its reason, the document links and period-close protection; no second movement or entry for the same event. Intermediate accounts such as GRNI are supported when goods arrive before the supplier invoice.
  * 7.6: numbering per company/branch/document type/fiscal year with prefixes and a concurrency-safe sequence; the official number is given at approval/issue; a cancelled number is never reused automatically.
  * 4.3/4.4: approval policies by document type, value, branch and risk; separation of duties; editing an approved-but-not-finally-posted document in material fields re-opens the approval cycle; a **posted** document is never edited or deleted (correction by an independent reversal or adjustment document); scheduled and automatic posting never bypass controls.
  * 19.2 one financial/stock effect per business event (idempotency); 19.3 no change/delete of a posted accounting document; 19.7 sales/purchase document states are separate from physical delivery and from actual accounting posting; 19.8 (via 3.10/3.11) full traceability of price, tax, exchange rate, cost and approval source.
* **Out of scope here (other phases):** concrete sales and purchase business flows and screens (3.15 purchasing, 3.16 sales), vouchers/cash/bank (3.14), receivables/payables and allocation (3.17), FX differences (3.18), cheques (3.19), recurring documents (3.21), printing/designer (3.26), e-invoicing (SRS 6.4).

## 3. Design principles (invariants, each proven by a test below)

1. **Three independent tracks, none derived by writing the others' flag.** Commercial status (what the business agreed), physical (what moved), accounting (what the ledger shows). Physical and accounting state are DERIVED from append-only event rows and the journal/stock facts; only commercial status is a stored column, changed by a guarded state machine.
2. **One effect per business event.** An event is a (document, event kind, sequence) triple with a deterministic key `commdoc:<document id>:<kind>:<n>`. The key is the unique source key of the stock posting, the costing operation and the journal entry, and the primary identity of the event row. A retry returns the original; concurrent requests produce exactly one effect.
3. **Posted means immutable.** A document that has an executed physical event or a posted/pending accounting effect cannot be edited, cancelled or deleted; correction is a separate linked return/reversal/adjustment document.
4. **Nothing is ever removed.** Documents, lines, links, events and numbers are never deleted (cancel/void only), PostgreSQL refuses UPDATE of identity and DELETE/TRUNCATE, like every module so far.
5. **Snapshots are values.** Price, tax, exchange rate and (when known) cost are stored as values with their hashes at the moment of the event; a later list/rule/rate change cannot alter or invalidate them.
6. **Derived, never stored, quantities.** Ordered, fulfilled, invoiced and returned quantities per line are computed from the link rows (append-only), so a counter can never drift; a constraint-backed check refuses over-fulfilment (Q3).
7. **Company-scoped and RBAC-checked server-side**; both clients use the same API; no frontend-only security.
8. **Fail closed.** Missing configuration (accounts, numbering rule, open period, rate) yields a refusal or an explicit pending state with a reason, never a guess.

## 4. Data model sketch (logical; opaque physical names generated by `tools/schema/company-0028-*.cjs`, same derivation, `--check` step and derivation tests as 0022-0027)

**Slice 1 (company 0028):**
* `CommercialDocument`: id; `DocType` (registry, see Q1); `Branch`; `Party` (customer or supplier role required); `Currency`; `DocumentDate`; `ExchangeRate` value and its source reference (dated rate or authorized override with reason); `Mode` (direct / separate, only meaningful for invoices; fixed after approval); `CommercialStatus` (1 draft, 2 submitted, 3 approved, 4 rejected, 5 cancelled; issued/closed states only if Q7/Q8 owner answers require them); `OfficialNumber` (null until approval; unique per type via the existing issue ledger); `FiscalYear`; totals (net, tax, gross, base-currency gross) as values; `CreatedBy/At`, `Revision` (bigint, +1 per change, optimistic concurrency), `IdempotencyKey` (client-supplied, unique per company, per document type and creator, used for create).
* `CommercialDocumentLine`: document, line number, item, unit, quantity, warehouse (null for pure commercial lines), `PricingSnapshot` (text + hash), `TaxSnapshot` (text + hash), line net/tax/gross values. Immutable after approval (a change is a new revision back through approval, see section 6).
* `CommercialDocumentEvent`: append-only history of commercial transitions (who, when, from/to, revision, reason, approval reference). The approval records reuse the stock-document pattern (independent approver bound to the exact revision).
* Triggers: identity and document type immutable; status transitions only along the state machine in SQL (a trigger refuses an impossible transition even for a direct statement); number immutable once set; lines immutable unless the document is draft; no DELETE/TRUNCATE.
* System migration (separate number, assigned by the lead at that time): permission codes proposal `company.commercialdocs.view|create|update|submit|approve|cancel`, plus later `post` and `reverse` in their slices; approver NOT in the preset Company Administrator (as the journal approver), delegated.

**Slice 2 (next company number, assigned then):** `DocumentLink` (append-only: from document and line, to document and line, kind: fulfils / invoices / returns / amends, quantity, creator/time; unique per (from line, to line, kind) and per idempotency key) and the derived-quantity views; a database check refuses a link that would push the fulfilled quantity of a source line above its ordered quantity (Q3 tolerance 0 by default) and a link between documents of different company, party, currency or incompatible types.

**Slice 3:** `DocumentPhysicalEvent` (append-only: document, kind delivered/received/returned-in/returned-out, sequence, stock document id (unique), idempotency key (unique), quantities per line in `DocumentPhysicalEventLine`); the stock document is created and approved through the existing stock services, executed through `StockPostingService`; derived physical state per line: none / partial / complete / over (refused).

**Slice 4:** `DocumentAccountingEvent` (append-only: document, event, journal entry id or "none", reason code, idempotency key (unique)); derived accounting state per document and event (pending / not required / posted / failed / reversed) by reading the journal entry (the 3.7C derivation); new period-close probe `commercial_accounting_pending`.

## 6. State machines and rules

**Commercial status (stored, SQL-guarded):** draft -> submitted -> approved (official number allocated here, Q7) -> (S3/S4 effects happen at approval or later, section 6.2); submitted -> rejected; draft/submitted/approved -> cancelled **only while no physical event and no accounting effect exists**; rejected -> draft (new revision). Cancelling an approved numbered document voids its number (never reused). A cancelled or rejected document is retained forever.

**Re-approval (SRS 4.4):** editing an approved document that has no executed physical event and no accounting effect, in a MATERIAL field (party, branch, currency, date, mode, any line item/unit/quantity/price/discount/tax/warehouse), moves it back to draft with a new revision; the previous approval stays in the history but is bound to the old revision and never counts for the new one; the official number is kept (not reallocated). Non-material fields (internal note, reference text) change in place and are audited. A document with a physical or accounting effect cannot be edited at all: only a linked return/reversal/adjustment document (S3/S4).

**6.2 Effects (S3/S4), exact rules from SRS 7.3:**
* **Direct mode (invoice):** approving the invoice creates ONE physical event (key `commdoc:<id>:physical:1`) that executes ONE stock document through `StockPostingService` in the SAME transaction as the approval (so approval cannot succeed without its stock effect, or fail leaving it half done), then the accounting effect (below).
* **Separate mode:** approving the invoice creates no stock effect. Approving a delivery/receipt document creates its physical event and stock effect; the invoice and the delivery are linked (S2); approving the invoice later creates **no** second stock movement and **no** second ledger effect for the delivered quantities (the invoice's own accounting is defined by Q4/Q5; the framework refuses any path that would post the same quantity twice, test below).
* **Accounting:** each event automatically creates one draft journal entry through the automatic path with key `commdoc:<id>:accounting:<event>`; if configuration or period state prevents it, an explicit pending state with a stable reason code is recorded (never silently skipped); period close is blocked while a pending effect remains (scenario 21.4); a Final Closed period is never posted into.
* **Foreign currency (ROADMAP):** a document is created and posted at its dated document rate (3.2B rates; manual override only with the existing permission, reason and audit); any settlement or allocation of such documents across currencies or rates is refused with code `fx_settlement_not_supported` until 3.18. S4 delivers the single gate function (the refusal and its test); 3.17 must call it.

## 7. Idempotency and the counting hook

* **Create idempotency:** `IdempotencyKey` unique per (company, document type, creator): a repeated create returns the original document with `replayed = true`; a key reused with a DIFFERENT payload is refused (`idempotency_key_reused`), exactly as the stock posting treats different amounts.
* **Event idempotency:** the deterministic event key of principle 2; the stock posting, costing operation, journal entry and the event row all carry it; replays return the originals.
* **Counting hook:** interface `IPostedBusinessTransactionCounter.RecordAsync(work, kind, sourceId)`, called INSIDE the posting transaction of every business document that reaches a posted/executed state, once per business event; the registered implementation is a no-op until 4.2 defines the Trial unit (OD-12). Tests: it is invoked exactly once per event, never on a replay, never for the automatic journal of a counted document or a reversal (the OD-12 proposal, recorded not decided), its absence of persistence (no-op leaves no row), and a throwing counter aborts the posting transaction (fail closed, nothing committed).

## 10. Risks and how the plan contains them

* **Duplicate effect under concurrency** is the principal risk: contained by deterministic event keys that are unique in three places (event row, stock posting/costing, journal source key), row locks on the document, and concurrency tests with real parallelism per slice.
* **Accounting rules invented by accident:** contained by Q4-Q6 defaults (explicit pending, no seeded mapping, no sales-return valuation) and by listing every such rule as OPEN.
* **Cross-slice drift of totals and snapshots:** snapshots stored as values with hashes and verified on storage.
* **Approval bypass by automatic tasks:** every automatic path goes through the same SQL approval gate and period gate; tested.
* **Scale of the change:** six slices, each independently reviewed against the full diff; no slice starts before its predecessor is merged; migration numbers are allocated by the lead per slice, never claimed in advance beyond 0028.

## 11. What is explicitly NOT assumed

No document type is activated for users, no account mapping is seeded, no revenue/COGS/GRNI posting rule is chosen, no sales-return valuation, no tolerance, no credit-limit enforcement, no Trial counting unit, and no screen: each is OPEN in section 8 until the owner decides, and the system refuses or marks pending rather than guessing.
