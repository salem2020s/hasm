# HASM — Phase 3.5: Stock movement documents up to approval (as built)

Sources: SRS §7 (document modes, "stock movement only for an approved physical handover event", official number at approval, voided numbers never reused), §9 (items, warehouses, cost per item × warehouse, FIFO and moving weighted average, negative stock policy, back-dated movements), ARCHITECTURE §7 (accounting principle: stock effect, ledger posting or an *explicit accounting-pending state* and audit in ONE atomic transaction) and §8 (costing engine), DECISIONS D-017/D-018/D-019, `docs/ROADMAP.md` (3.1C warehouses, 3.4/3.4B inventory master data).
The roadmap assigns no number to the stock phase; the next free number **3.5** is used.

## Decision: what is posted in this phase, and what is not
A physical stock effect may only be committed atomically with its **valuation** and its **accounting state** (ARCHITECTURE §7). A receipt must create a FIFO layer or move the weighted average (it needs a cost); an issue must consume layers or the average to produce the cost of the goods; a transfer must carry the actual cost to the receiving warehouse (D-018); a back-dated movement needs an impact preview and recalculation (SRS §9). **None of the costing engine (FIFO layers, average ledger, per item × warehouse locks, recalculation) exists yet.** A quantity-only posting would create stock without a cost, would have to be rewritten, and would make "quantity on hand" and "inventory valuation" disagree. Therefore:

* **Delivered now (this phase):** the document model and life cycle of receipts, issues and transfers **up to approval**. An approved document is only an approved intention: official number, fiscal year, approver. **No quantity, balance, movement ledger, cost layer or accounting entry is created or changed by any operation of this phase**, and no table for them exists (asserted by tests). The UI says so on every screen. The close check `inventory_valuation_differences` therefore stays "not applicable".
* **Next slices (not started, properly bounded, in dependency order):** **3.6 costing engine** (moving weighted average and FIFO per item × warehouse, concurrency control, no-negative-stock rules, tracked items) → **3.7 posting** (an approved document becomes immutable movements atomically with its cost and its accounting state; opening quantities; cancellation/reversal of posted documents; back-dated impact preview) → quantity-on-hand screens and the inventory reports. Negative stock stays refused by default; FIFO and lot/serial items can never go negative; a controlled moving-average exception needs its own approved design (provisional cost + later settlement) and is not introduced here.

## What exists
* **Warehouses (3.1C, reused, completed):** creation, edit, deactivation, unique codes, Arabic/English names, branch relationship, Trial limit (one warehouse), warehouse-scoped RBAC and audit already existed. This phase adds the dependency guard: a warehouse with **open documents** (draft, submitted, approved) cannot be deactivated (`warehouse_has_open_documents`, serialised against document creation by the same structure lock); a document can only use an active warehouse; PostgreSQL still refuses deleting any warehouse.
* **Company migration 0013** (`CompanySchema.StockDocuments.cs`): `StockDocument`, `StockDocumentLine`, `StockDocumentEvent`. PostgreSQL enforces: immutable type/branch/creation data; never deleted or truncated; the status graph (1 draft → 2 submitted → 3 approved; 2 → 1 withdrawn; 2 → 4 rejected; 1, 2, 3 → 5 cancelled; rejected and cancelled final); header and lines editable only while a draft; at least one line once submitted (deferred check); warehouses fit the type (receipt target only, issue source only, transfer two different warehouses); **separation of duties** (the decider is never the submitter, also against direct SQL); the official number exists exactly while approved and is kept on cancellation and unique per type; `base quantity = quantity × factor` exactly; append-only history linked to one authenticated audit event each. Runtime role: SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE (DELETE only on lines, which PostgreSQL lets change only in a draft).
* **System migration 0016** and six warehouse-scoped permissions `company.stockdocs.view|create|update|submit|approve|cancel`; the Company Administrator preset holds them all. A company-wide grant covers every warehouse, a warehouse grant only that warehouse (no inheritance from branches); a document needs the permission on **every** warehouse it involves (a transfer on both), viewing on at least one; documents of other warehouses are neither listed nor disclosed.
* **Numbering:** three document types (`stock_receipt` SR, `stock_issue` SI, `stock_transfer` ST) in the existing numbering engine (per type, optionally per branch/fiscal year; default `SR-2026-000001`). The number is issued **at approval** inside the approval transaction (no gaps on rollback); cancelling an approved document marks its number void, which is never issued again. Stock types are excluded from the ordered approval-policy engine (journal-only).
* **Life cycle** (`StockDocumentService`): create, edit (draft), submit, withdraw to draft, **approve** (independent approver; the document date must lie in an **open** fiscal period; number issued), reject (note), cancel (reason; draft, submitted or approved). Every transition is fenced by the revision (`If-Match`): a duplicate or concurrent request fails with 412 and does nothing twice; every transition writes one audit event (1140–1146) and one history row in the same transaction; references (warehouses, items, units) are validated again at submit and approve.
* **Lines and conversion:** item, unit, quantity; the factor to the item's base unit is read from the item at that moment and stored on the line; base quantity = quantity × factor in exact decimal arithmetic; a quantity not representable in the entered unit's decimals or a base quantity not representable in the base unit's decimals is **refused, never rounded**. Services, inactive items/units, units not defined for the item and **items tracked by lot, serial or expiry** are refused (lot/serial identification on lines does not exist yet: such items cannot be moved without their tracking). Transfers between warehouses of different branches are refused (`transfer_cross_branch_not_supported`: inter-branch accounting does not exist yet). Cross-company references are impossible (one database per company) and unknown ids are `not_found`.
* **API** `/api/v1/company/stock-documents` (capabilities, warehouses, items lookup, list with filters and paging, get with lines and history, create, update, submit, withdraw, approve, reject, cancel); `no-store`, anti-CSRF, `If-Match`.
* **Shared UI (Web and Tauri):** `StockDocuments.tsx` under Inventory → *Stock documents*: list with filters (type, status, warehouse, dates, number/reference) and paging; the editor uses the permanent tabs standard (General, Lines, History); the identity (number, status) and the workflow actions stay outside the tabs; unsaved edits survive tab switches; errors mark their tab; Submit asks for confirmation and is disabled while edits are unsaved; Approve/Reject are disabled with the reason for the submitter; Reject and Cancel need a note/reason. Arabic and English, RTL/LTR.

## Limits and open items
No posting, quantity on hand, valuation or accounting (see the decision above). No lot/serial/expiry lines, no negative-stock exception, no cross-branch transfers, no document attachments, no approval-policy amounts/levels (the independent-approver rule is the whole approval), no reservation. Posting of approved documents, cancellation of *posted* documents (the guard slot exists: cancelling is refused once a posting exists), costing, quantity screens and inventory reports belong to the next slices.
